Attractions

Pikes Peak Cog Railway cograilway.com ↗

The highest cog railway in the world — 3.5-hr round trip to the 14,115-ft summit of America's Mountain. The aspens turn in mid-October and the summit may have snow. Seats are reserved and advance purchase is recommended.

$67+/person Advance tickets advised
The Broadmoor Seven Falls sevenfalls.com ↗

Seven cascading waterfalls in a 1,400-ft box canyon. Easy 0.8-mile walk to the base, with staircases and an elevator to an upper observation deck. Free shuttle from parking. Zip lining available separately.

~$20/person Advance tickets advised
Cheyenne Mountain Zoo cmzoo.org ↗

America's only mountain zoo at 6,714 feet. Rated #2 Best Zoo in North America (2025). Runs a Halloween event on select October evenings — could be a fun group activity if timing lines up.

~$30/person
U.S. Olympic & Paralympic Museum usopm.org ↗

Hands-on exhibits and interactive sports stations — test your speed, accuracy, and reaction time. One of the most praised museums in Colorado Springs. Easy to pair with a downtown lunch.

~$25/person
Ghost Town Museum ghosttownmuseum.com ↗

A walk-through recreation of a Colorado gold rush town — restored buildings, artifacts, hands-on exhibits. Low-key and fun. Good for a couple of relaxed hours between bigger activities.

~$10/person

Outdoor & scenery

Garden of the Gods gardenofgods.com ↗

1,341-acre National Natural Landmark with towering red sandstone formations — Balanced Rock, Gateway Rock, and more. Visitor center has exhibits, a café, and great views of Pikes Peak. A Colorado Springs essential.

Free entry
Red Rock Canyon Open Space City park info ↗

A quieter alternative to Garden of the Gods. Large red sandstone formations, hiking and biking trails through the Pikes Peak foothills. Less crowded, more rugged. Free city park.

Free entry
North Cheyenne Cañon Park City park info ↗

Rugged canyon hiking, Helen Hunt Falls, and the Starsmore Discovery Center. A local favorite that gets overlooked by tourists. Beautiful fall foliage in October. Free entry.

Free entry
Pikes Peak Highway (self-drive) pikespeakcolorado.com ↗

Drive yourself to the 14,115-ft summit in your own vehicle. Same views as the Cog, more flexibility, lower cost. A good complement or alternative depending on what we decide.

~$15/vehicle

Neighborhoods & culture

Manitou Springs manitousprings.org ↗

Quirky mountain town with art galleries, the famous natural mineral spring fountains, and good independent restaurants. About 10 minutes from downtown. Pairs naturally with a Pikes Peak or Seven Falls day.

Free to explore
Old Colorado City shopoldcoloradocity.com ↗

Historic district lined with galleries, boutiques, and independent restaurants. Great for a relaxed afternoon stroll or a first-evening orientation after everyone lands. October is Arts Month in the Springs.

Free to browse
U.S. Air Force Academy usafa.af.mil ↗

One of the country's most distinctive military academy campuses. Cadet Chapel, Falcon Stadium, Arnold Hall. A new visitor center outside the gate opened May 2026 — no base access required.

Free
Downtown ghost tour (evening) Ghost tours ↗

Walking tour of historic downtown — past the Mining Exchange, Acacia Park, and The Gold Room. Spooky history and local legends. A natural fit for the Halloween season and a fun group evening activity.

~$20/person

Dinner — nicer nights out

The Warehouse Restaurant thewarehouserestaurant.com ↗

New American in an art-filled industrial space downtown. Chef James Africano's menu spotlights local game and seasonal ingredients — think Thai Peanut Braised Yak and Grilled Bison Rib Eye. Inventive and memorable.

$$$
MacKenzie's Chop House mackenzies.com ↗

Classic Colorado chop house — steaks, seafood, extensive wine list and martini menu. Beautiful patio with twinkling lights. One of the most consistently recommended fine dining spots in the city.

$$$
Four by Brother Luck brotherluck.com ↗

Seasonal four-course tasting menu by Bravo TV celebrity chef Brother Luck. Multiple selections per course — you build your own progression. An experience as much as a meal. Good for a group that enjoys food as an event.

$$$$
Carlos Bistro carlosbistro.com ↗

Intimate upscale American — owner Carlos is known for greeting guests personally. A local hidden gem. Bison short ribs and herb-crusted salmon are standouts. Exceptional service.

$$$
Prime 25 prime25cs.com ↗

Modern steakhouse south of downtown — two stories, copper finishes, glass fireplaces, cozy lighting. A special-occasion feel without being stuffy. Good for a group celebration dinner.

$$$

Dinner — casual & fun

The Rabbit Hole therabbitholecos.com ↗

Whimsical decor and a hidden entrance — feels like stepping into a different world. Creative cocktails, eclectic menu. One of the most distinctive atmospheres in the city for a group night out.

$$
Tokki tokkicos.com ↗

Asian tapas, cozy booths, sultry lighting, live music, and excellent cocktails. Frequently named one of the top date night spots in the city. Great for a group that wants lively atmosphere and shareable plates.

$$
The Golden Bee broadmoor.com ↗

An English pub imported piece by piece from Britain, inside The Broadmoor resort. Lively sing-along atmosphere on evenings. Communal tables, great beer selection. A fun and memorable group experience.

$$
Phantom Canyon Brewing phantomcanyon.com ↗

Craft brewery in a beautifully restored Victorian building downtown. Great beers, solid pub food, casual and relaxed. Good for a low-key group dinner without the fuss of a reservation.

$$
The Margarita at Pine Creek margaritaatpinecreek.com ↗

New Mexican cuisine in a warm rustic setting with live music (acoustic, jazz). A bit off the beaten path but consistently praised for atmosphere and food. Great for a relaxed evening with good conversation.
